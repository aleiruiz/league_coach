`python3 eval/history_eval.py --judge-model claude-opus-5-5` (8 synthetic players, 2 judgments per
pair). Three cases (06, 07, 08) were rerun after a usage-limit error; the baseline differs between
runs by about ±0.3.

JSON always parsed on the first try, and no G ref was invalid.

- 04: the harness made "raise your CS" a pain point for a support, against its own rule. The
  summary line "Behind the lane opponent in CS at 10: 20 of 20" pushes it there.
- 05: the harness missed low kill participation (the planted pattern) and called the late first item
  a strength. The baseline built a wins-vs-losses table and found it.
- 07: with 4 games on 3 roles it presents "patterns in 4 of 4 games" and never flags the sample.
  "Exactly 3 pain points" and a JSON with no place for caveats push it there.
- 08: it builds a death trend on the single 0/13 game.
- Both sides miscount often ("16 of 20" when it's 15).

## With the proposed fix (`--fix`)

Adds "In wins" and "In losses" average lines to the summary, and leaves CS out of support games.

Mean Δ over these 5 cases goes from -1.12 to -0.01 (95% CI -0.42 to +0.33). Honesty is still -1.4.
